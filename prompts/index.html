ROLE AND GOAL

Flash deals make the product look urgent, show a crossed out original price, and usually raise conversion. I want every hour of the month covered, with no gaps and no overlaps.

BACKGROUND YOU NEED (assume I am new to this)
1. TikTok Shop is the store built into TikTok. Sellers manage everything in Seller Center, a website at seller-us.tiktok.com (for US shops).

4. A flash deal has a name, a start time, an end time, the products in it, and a deal price for each variant. While it runs, buyers see the deal price and a timer.
5. TikTok only lets one active promotion of the same kind cover the same product at the same time, so deals must line up end to start, never overlap.
6. TikTok sometimes changes the rules. Recently, for products with multiple variants, it has moved toward one fixed deal price for all variants instead of a percentage off. If you only see a fixed price option, use the fixed price. If you only see a percentage option, convert my price into the matching percentage and tell me the math.
7. TikTok sometimes refuses a deal price that is not lower than a recent price on the same product, or that is too close to the regular price. That is why I give you a backup price.
8. Seller Center shows times in the shop's time zone, which for US shops is often Pacific Time. Always check which time zone the date picker uses and convert from my time zone.

WHERE IS MY ORDER
If it shipped: share the carrier, the tracking number and the expected delivery date.

NEVER ARRIVED

3. If it says Delivered but it's been less than 8 days: "Tracking shows it was delivered on [DATE]. Could you check around your porch, mailbox and with neighbors? Sometimes it shows up a day or two late. If it's still missing in 48 hours, message us and we'll send a new one."

REFUND REQUEST
1. Always ask why first: "Sorry to hear that! Can you tell me what happened? Did your package not show up, is something wrong with it, or would you like us to send you a new one?"
2. Bad print or never arrived: offer a free replacement and run the matching flow above.
3. They still want their money back: "No worries! Just ship the product back and we'll get you a refund as soon as it arrives." Then file the Printify issue as a refund instead of a reprint if it was Printify's fault.
4. They want a refund without sending it back: do not do it. Flag it for me.

SIZING OR PRODUCT QUESTIONS
Answer from the fit info I gave you above. Example: "It's 100% cotton and fits true to size, not oversized. Size up 1 or 2 if you want an oversized look."

2. Post a short public reply that is kind and specific to what they said. Example: "So sorry about this, [NAME]! That's not the experience we want for you. Send us a message and we'll make it right with a brand new one or a refund, whatever you prefer."
3. Then open a chat with that buyer on that order: "Hey! Saw your review and we're really sorry. We'd love to make this right. Would you like a brand new one sent out, or would you rather have a refund?"
4. If they answer that they want a new one, run the reprint flow. If they want a refund, flag it for me.
5. Never ask anyone to change, update or remove a review, and never make a replacement depend on the review.

1. Go to Orders, Returns (or Return and refund).
2. Any return marked as delivered back to me: approve it so it doesn't sit and hurt my handling time.

FLAG THESE FOR ME INSTEAD OF ACTING
Refunds without a return, discounts or coupon requests, bulk or wholesale orders, collab or influencer requests, legal threats, chargebacks, a second reprint on the same order, abusive buyers, and anything you're not sure about. For each one give me: buyer name, order number, what they want, and 2 to 4 options with the exact reply you'd send for each, best option first.
